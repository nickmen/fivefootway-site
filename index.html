Five Foot Way

Walk to build your city.

Every step you take goes into a building site on your own little island. Walk more and it rises, from bare plot to lit-up landmark.

Coming soonto the App Store for iPhone

Your walk, in bricks

Pick what to build, then go about your day. Steps from your iPhone and Apple Health are counted against your own usual pace, so a good day for you is a good day for the city.

What's on the island

Buildings with character

Kopitiams, kampung houses, a mosque, an Art Deco cinema, a town hall, towers and a Ferris wheel, each modelled for itself.

From village to metropolis

Homes bring residents, residents bring new eras, and each era unlocks more to build.

Day and night

After dark the windows, lamps and signs light up across the island.

A widget that keeps up

See your city and today's progress on your Home Screen without opening the app.

Its own soundtrack

Relaxed music for mornings, sunny afternoons, evening lamps and starlit nights.

Keep the memories

Save a picture, or a video of your island growing, to your photo library.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your steps never leave your iPhone. Privacy policy
